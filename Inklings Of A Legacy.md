Chapter 1: Frustration  
*"Exeggutor, Psybeam now\! Aim at a fourty-five degree angle just as you hit the ceiling. Aim for Glaceon just as Terrakion throws it in the air using X-Scissor\!"*

Wolfe's command proceeded to careen outwards towards the stands as the Coconut Pokémon flew into the air, having just been tossed up by Glaceon's dreadlocks. Its Sitrus Berry hung around one of its many leaves as the Grass-Type nodded, beginning to gather pinkish-violet energy amidst the foliage atop its body, just like how a plant gathered energy through photo-synthesis.

Yet as its feet pushed off the roof of the Complex's stadium, the lighting system jostled from ice particles that shook it at the same time, causing the short Pokémon to lose its balance and plummet towards stage-center..  

Wolfe's eyes widened in both bewilderment and terror as his plan seemed to unravel before him, merely watching as the Psybeam fired towards the Fresh Snow Pokémon just as the Fighting type had positioned itself like a catapult. And yet Wolfe's Terrakion grinned with vengeance as it began readying the Ice-type above its two brown tusks which were both turning a matching forest green. 

Wolfe nodded toward the Sword of Justice as it heaved the small Ice type upwards while the sound of Glaceon's dreadlocks rustled amidst the now cooler air. Seconds passed before an explosion shook the stadium, its crowd shaken to a chilling silence that matched the almost glacial air that had taken hold of the venue itself.

 Even Clay, who had been watching from the audience, took a deep breath before the coal miner briefly took off his cowboy hat only to look at the Dawn Stone that was engraved into it, only to put it back in its proper place atop his head as a sign of respect for the Pokémon.

As the smoke from the impact cleared overhead, heads with mixed expressions watched in shared disarray. In fact, aside from Driftveil's residential Gym Leader–who quickly steeled like the underlying patience of a Krookodile, everyone was in a panic looking for the Glaceon and the battle's conclusion\!

Yet the announcement of a Pokémon being 'unable to battle' never came. Two thoughts came up amongst the crowd's murmurs as only Clay's eyes properly scanned the battle, looking for clues like a Dowsing Machine would. Yet he found nothing. 

Meanwhile the rest of the crowd began murmuring more, hushed voices in worry turning into a tempest. Where was Mightyena? Was Glaceon alright? Clearly the crowd all had Unaware as their Ability, thought the Gym Leader with a soft chuckle. Yet almost immediately after the laugh did his mouth slam shut, his eyes suddenly sharpening with the same ferocity which surveyed Gym battles with multiple a youngin' on their journey. 

However this was *no* mere Gym Battle.

What was immediately clear to Clay was the crowd's apparent stupidity. What they all missed in their frantic searches, was the sound of paws pattering in panic just moments prior to the beam's impact. It was as if the Mightyena had been running to protect its partner.

"The little 'un's fine\! Pay attention you darn fools\!" Clay called out as an attempt to calm down the crowd. 

 Yet this did nothing to calm the tempest of the venue, their mountain of murmurs only growing louder by the minute. Clay's teeth clenched in a bit of anger as he looked on towards the field.

What ended up silencing the crowd's murmuring, was a landing on the field. 

Both Pokémon, relatively unharmed aside from bruises attained prior in the battle, pressed onto the field in sync, the eyes of the Bite Pokémon venomous with rage as both partners now stood the field to the crowd's unanimous uproar\!

The Bite Pokémon's eyes locked on towards its ally's assailant. Without thinking, it began sprinting towards the now falling Exeggutor at a breakneck pace, its steps ever so quiet as it thundered towards the air with a large leap. It grabbed the Exeggutor by the leaf, simply holding it in place while embers began to dance amongst its now combusting fangs. Hilbert nodded as if in understanding before proceeding to call out the final command of the match.

"Mightyena, use Fire Fang on Exeggutor and Terrakion once Glaceon uses Ice Fang to throw it into the air\!"

At their trainer’s call, both Pokemon rushed toward their opponents as Mightyena’s canines began to spontaneously aduresce with flame, enough to quickly mar the Bite Pokémon’s maw with fire that burnt a deep mix of both orange and red hues.

At the same time, the fangs of its partner had begun to algesce at the incisors, its now rapidly dropping body temperature causing them to turn a light cyan; If anything, the Fresh Snow Pokemon seemed almost elated while running, its teeth embracing the familiar temperatures of absolute-zero like a warm blanket.

 But Wolfe didn't hear this play, see his Pokémon faint, nor witness the announcement that he had lost, his footsteps having already decided to exit the stadium in failure.

As he was let out, Wolfe grabbed the two Ultra Balls at his sides, unclipping them from the belt around his jeans. He held the black spheres outward, his voice struggling to feign calmness amidst his frustration.

 “Terrakion, Exeggutor, return\!” 

Two red lights, small yet focused, proceeded to shoot out of the devices with their trajectories aimed towards the Pokémon who lay likely fainted on the field. Merely a few seconds passed before Wolfe had reclipped the Ultra Balls to his belt, leaning his arms against the wall as his mind replayed the events in his mind like a bell calling children to supper.

*Two times in the same year. Just perfect\!* Wolfe let out a groan, letting the *memory* fading from his mind like it was never there. After all, it was a fluke he decided.  “Should’ve had Terrakion flinch them with Rock Slide while I had the chance earlier. It was right there,”  he reminded himself this with a colder tone, even as his tone began to take on a venomous quality.

Yet these thoughts were silenced as his body began to suddenly shiver; He deduced as he walked that it was likely due to the sheer amount of energy he had expended during the tournament proper.

And yet, he wondered if he had tried hard enough. He almost had it. The thoughts came back suddenly as if welcome within his mind.

Despite these thoughts weighing him down, Wolfe continued to walk confidently through the registration area, weaving though the crowd of former audience members like butter. And as he finally stepped out into the stinging light of Driftveil, he went to observe a Pawniard, the way the small Pokémon handled its blade-esque appendages seemed to fascinate the young trainer. 

But he knew why he was doing it, thinking that this would help his stress. And yet as he sat down with the currently fading thoughts of his prior battle with Hilbert, they were, were ripped from his mind once he thought of what typing both the Bite and Sharp Blade Pokémon had in common.

*They were both **Dark** **types.***

Suddenly, he stopped observing, only able to watch as *a faint memory of a battle with a Hydreigon flashed into his mind, the multiple heads, its verdantly forest-green body, the purple scales. How they moved across the field like a skilled orchestra playing a piece. No…everything was becoming clearer. He…  He could literally see the…The… Three draconic heads, the sound of its six wings flapping, all working in sync to keep the Brutal Pokémon aloft. Suddenly he stood opposite a stadium. He could hear the crowd and their cheering… And then almost at once his arms moved, the Coconut Pokémon and the Cavern Pokémon returning to their Ultra Balls in thin red lights. He heard the referee, watched their hand move in a slicing motion, “Both Exeggutor and Terrakion are unable to battle\! Thus, the winner, and our still-reigning World Champion of the Xurkitree Circuit is—*” 

Before the referee could finish, the crowd's uproar took over. Suddenly, Wolfe could feel a subtle stinging at his neck as he snapped forward, his hands now shaking vicariously. Yet the small samurai-like Pokémon walked over towards him seeming entranced with worry and curiosity

Yet, Wolfe wasn’t focusing on this act of simple kindness the Pokémon was giving him.. 

Instead he looked at his palms. Upon further inspection, he could see lines of sweat beginning to form across the creases. It pissed him off, especially now that his neck had a damn pink welt marked by Solgaleo of all things\! Wasn't he in Unova?\!

 “This is why I hate dragons…” He stated to himself begrudgingly, finally remembering the loss at Manalo Stadium vividly but forgetting the trainer’s name. However the sun’s rays, which had caused the sunburn gave him an idea he didn’t wish to think of any further.

As Wolfe slowly began to slow his now racing heart, first getting up from his seat to toussle his own hair, he initially was extremely put off by the memory. Of course he now had the impression that he had trained his Pokémon incorrectly… Just perfect\! He set his head down onto the table with a groan; his mind was belligerent, as if mocking him every second. Unbeknownst to Wolfe however, the Still Blade Pokémon had walked off, seemingly disinterested in its playmate.

Wolfe ignored the Pokémon completely before he pushed it, and the matches from the sight of his mind. After a bit of indecision as to how to avoid the crowd, he eventually decided to begin walking around, eventually deciding to lean against a lamppost at some point in order to stay out of the crowd for as long as possible. After all, he had to control himself\!

“You’re not there… At all.” He repeated to himself, “It’s fine to lose. People won’t remember that, of course not. It’s…” He repeated the words that played into his mind, yet over time those same words began to scatter away from him. Yet four words came to his mind that he had decided, in that moment, were temporary but useful.

“It’s okay to lose.” 

An entire hour passed in silence, with nothing but this mantra Wolfe muttered to himself occasionally breaking it. A flock of Pidove flew above the stadium, the Tiny Dove Pokémon all being led by both an Unfeazant and Tranquil who all engulfed the area in shrill chirps that followed the gentle breeze. 

Meanwhile, Wolfe had begun digging into his bag, in need of a snack that would supply him with the proper strength needed for his travels. 

After a bit of rummaging, he pulled out a still-frigid ice cream cone from Castelia—only possible thanks to Colress's modifications to his bag, which could now regulate its temperature based on what each section required. It was because of this one moment that his opinion on the Unovan metropolis had changed. 

After Wolfe finished ensuring everything ELSE was in order, primarily the health and wellbeing of his Pokémon, he let out a breath of relief.

Now seeming content, he continued on his way to the city's pier, all the while biting down into his ice cream while idly observing multiple species of Pokémon simply just living their daily lives. It was odd to him. So many Pokémon living in peace when he had been accustomed to purely seeing them as sport. As he simply observed in peace, he had a thought. And a confusing one at that

*Had he forgotten that Pokémon were humans too? Was that why he had lost? There had to be a connection there.*

But this thought didn't last as he stilled almost immediately, the rest of the area seeming to follow suit when a distinct sound began to overtake the area. 

The sound of rain clouds rolling in almost randomly, followed by the sound of thunder. He could recognize that sound anywhere, especially as the temperature suddenly dropped and his clothes began getting soaked due to the heavy rain. Wait a second… Heavy rain, along with thunder right now? This was *obviously* planned.  

“There’s no way there’s someone battling here\!” Wolfe noted with excited curiosity. His mood seemed to shift ever so slightly, curious at the thought of a way to improve himself. 

*Observation.*

With little delay, Wolfe took to asking the surrounding passerby and vendors, which, in due course, led him to a battle that was about to happen out in the open. Two trainers, standing on opposite sides, threw out a Pokémon each. Wolfe, who was tunneled in on the battle’s result only noticed the Turtle Pokémon and neglected the other opponent entirely until the battle progressed further because of… something minute. The lack of a Light Ball on the Pikachu. This confused him. Wouldn't it be far weaker without it?

Yet, Wolfe was quickly proven wrong once the   Turtle Pokémon used Dig to avoid an incoming Thunderbolt, a crater having now taken the place of where the lightning had struck the ground’s surface..

Up to this point, Pikachu’s movements were jagged, snapping across the ground unnaturally fast like a metronome keeping time. Yet speed like that usually belonged to a Pokémon like Electrode, or a Bug type like Ninjask— not a Pikachu of all things. And it hitting so hard without a Light Ball? That STILL threw him off-kilter\!

Wolfe’s pen proceeded to run across the page, the thought bleeding itself into the paper in scrawled bursts. ‘*Naturally limber? Could be?. Agility? Likely but less than it being naturally limber; Would help speed. But usually Pokémon use four moves. Maybe wasteful? Doesn’t make sense.’*

He tapped the pen against his notebook, a nervous rhythm against the cardboard before his eyes went back and forth between paper and battle. ‘*Could it know Rapid Spin? No. That’s Wartortle’s trick. Are Pikachu even able to spin that fast?\!’*

And yet—Wolfe knew instinctively that something had allowed Pikachu to dive after Wartortle into the tunnels. Wolfe scribbled fast, half-listening to the muffled exchange beneath the dirt: the sharp *crackling* of lightning from Pikachu’s sacs, or the muffled *hissing* of Ice Beam. The crowd—including Wolfe, all murmured in unison, a restless wave of sound from those who couldn't see the action underground.

Then a collective gasp rippled through them as the Mouse Pokémon shot from the tunnel, ice climbing its legs as the Turtle Pokémon flew out next,its prior usage of Rapid Spin causing its hard shell to whir like an Excadrill using Drill Run. 

Wolfe continued watching intently with a notebook as the familiar yellow blur proceeded to speed across the field, its tail that distinct jagged yellow. Yet the trainer’s final command solidified the battle’s end and sold Wolfe’s intrigue on him.

 “Pikachu, catch yourself in the air then send Wartortle flying using Iron Tail\!\!”

The Mouse Pokémon proceeded to follow this order with an interesting flair, appearing below the Turtle Pokémon and first just staying below it, merely giggling as it tossed Wartortle up and down, currently using its tail like a baseball player would a bat.

This remained the case for but a minute. At least before Wartortle was, was finally slammed into the air\! The Electric type then used Quick Attack, the same crackling sound Wolfe had heard prior accompanying its rapid movements before it appeared above its opponent, slamming its now ferrous tail into the brown shell of the Water type. This, as Wolfe expected, caused the Turtle Pokémon to careen towards the ground and finally make impact with the ground like a doomed meteor. 

The referee took a quick look at the result before their hand sliced downward. Their hand then moved to the left as they announced triumphantly, “Wartortle is unable to battle\! Ash Ketchum is the winner\!”

Chapter II: Endeavor  
It was often said that beneath the bustling city of Castelia that there was a fighting ring. Not set up by people, but by Pokemon themselves, having been created a few years after the city's inception. Often Trainers would end up confused by the scratches and bruising that their Pokemon would receive, stating that their Pokemon just "wandered off". 

But the Nurse Joy that was stationed there had seen it all–usually deep cuts or abrasions across the body in some places, in some blood. Her blue eyes told stories that most people often didn't question, nor ask. Audino had seen it as well, the Hearing Pokemon's mind having been ravaged by such things even before she'd been taken in by the nurse. 

Suddenly the automatic doors swung open as the Nurse peered over the counter, watching an orange blur quickly rush through the Center only to steal three yellow Sitrus Berries, the objects of nourishment large against its back as it ran out before she or Audino could hit the alarm, its crimson button hidden under the similarly red counter. Yet even while the sun peered in from beyond the Center, replacing skies similar to that Pokemon's fur with one's akin to a Lapras' smooth body, she recalled its blurry and distinctly jagged tail–barely able to be seen amidst its hurried steps. 

Nurse Joy sighed slightly, putting her head to her head, shaking it ever so after the doors closed. 

*What had happened to that poor Raichu, she thought.*

And yet, at the same time that the Nurse Joy had noticed the light stains of blood on the carpet floor had the Raichu banked a second right. 

J*ust* *go straight*, its mind told it before it began looking behind itself as best it could--not THAT it could of course, the berries made it hard to run amidst Castelia's sunlight, but the large structures adorning the city did help. A bit.

It'd already avoided that Krookodile once before, but suddenly the ground began a light tremor before the Raichu was lifted ten feet in the air on... some type of rock formation, its jagged surfaces allowing the berries to cleanly roll down to the ground. Almost immediately did the Raichu's eyes widen, its yellow sacs filling with electricity at once. Its tail, which usually would be more vibrant with cockiness or anger, slithering like an Ekans-- never did with this Raichu...

It usually ran... didn't enjoy fighting. But considering what the others would do with it if they found it...? It immediately found itself gulping with fear, recalling a memory of its tail having been tied up against a girder as an Electivire proceeded to shock it repeatedly to the point of vomiting.

It couldn't back up, it'd plummet to its death... Maybe make its tail like the walls of that 'building' it exited, through the 'door'? Well, here goes nothing, the Electric type thought. But just as the Mouse Pokemon's tail sharpened into a sharp silver, now refracting the light of the rising sun did a maroon, scaled hand grabbed two of the berries before biting into them with gusto; the Intimidation Pokemon letting out a deep guttural snarl as its eyes, pitch black, proceeded to stab holes into the fellow Pokémon.

The sight, or rather the realization itself that its back was against the wall– was, was what caused the Raichu's face to pale to a ghastly white, its body beginning to tremble at the seams.

*It had been found.*

*It… It had been found.*

*Again*. 

Instinctively, the Mouse Pokémon’s ears drooped, its head lowering in defeat as the Krookodile’s slow steps began towards the orange Electric-type Pokemon, its claws radiated a deep jagged turquoise. 

A Dragon type’s claw. When did the boss.. learn their ways? How?\!

*Fight back.*
